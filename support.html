Quick answers

The app is asking for Camera / Photos permission — why?

Camera permission is requested only when you tap "Attach photo" on a transaction. Photos permission is requested only when you save a portfolio snapshot. You can decline either — the rest of the app continues to work fully.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, locally. CoinhakoSphere does not transmit your transactions, holdings, or alerts to any server operated by the developer.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings → Export to CSV from inside the app. You will receive a CSV file containing your full ledger that you can save to Files, share via Mail, or send to yourself.

How do I delete everything?

Delete CoinhakoSphere from your iPhone. Because all data is stored locally inside the app, removing the app removes your data. There is no remote account to deactivate.

Does CoinhakoSphere place trades or connect to exchanges?

No. CoinhakoSphere is strictly a journaling tool. It cannot move funds, place orders, or read exchange accounts.
