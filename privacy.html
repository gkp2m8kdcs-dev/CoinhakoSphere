Privacy Policy

How CoinhakoSphere handles your data.

Support · Privacy · Terms of Use

Effective date: September 21, 2026
App: CoinhakoSphere · Platform: iOS (iPhone)

Summary in plain language

CoinhakoSphere is a private, on-device crypto investment journal. Your transactions, holdings, watchlist, and price alerts live only on your iPhone. The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not transmit your ledger to any server operated by us.

Information we collect

CoinhakoSphere does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data from you.

The only information stored by the app is the data you voluntarily enter:

  • Transactions you record (type, asset, amount, price, date, notes).
  • Holdings, watchlist items, and price alerts you create.
  • App preferences such as base currency and theme.

This information is stored locally on your device using standard iOS data storage. It is not sent to a remote server operated by the developer.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No account, no email address, no login credentials.
  • No wallet addresses, exchange API keys, or private keys.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • No location data, contacts, or device fingerprint.
  • No crash reports tied to your identity.
  • No analytics that identify you personally.

Market data

To display current prices and charts, the app fetches public market data from third-party cryptocurrency data providers. These requests are made by iOS on behalf of the app and contain only the standard technical information that every internet request exposes (such as IP address and timestamp). No personal identifier, no entry from your ledger, and no analytics payload is attached to those requests.

Third-party services

CoinhakoSphere relies on standard Apple iOS frameworks and public market data endpoints. We do not integrate any third-party SDKs that profile users. If Apple frameworks (such as App Store, system notifications, or iCloud) process any data, they do so under Apple's own privacy terms.

iCloud and device backups

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your iPhone, an encrypted copy of CoinhakoSphere's local data may be included in your personal iCloud backup. This backup is governed by Apple's iCloud terms and is accessible only to you with your Apple ID credentials. The developer has no access to your iCloud account or its contents.

Permissions requested by the app

  • Camera: only if you choose to attach a photo to a transaction. The image is stored locally with the transaction.
  • Photo Library: only if you choose to save a portfolio snapshot to your photos.

CoinhakoSphere does not request access to your contacts, microphone, location, Face ID, or push notifications.

Children's privacy

CoinhakoSphere is intended for users 16 years of age or older (or older where mandated by local law). The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights and choices

  • Access & portability: export your full ledger as CSV from inside the app at any time.
  • Deletion: delete the app to permanently remove all locally stored data. There is no remote account to close because no account was ever created.
  • Corrections: edit or delete any transaction, holding, watchlist item, or alert directly in the app.

Data security

CoinhakoSphere relies on Apple's iOS sandboxing and on-device storage protections. Because your data never leaves your device, the risk of remote data exposure is minimized. You are responsible for keeping your iPhone secured (passcode, Face ID, Find My iPhone) just as you would with any personal data on the device.

Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will also be communicated through an in-app notice where appropriate.
