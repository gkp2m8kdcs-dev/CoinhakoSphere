Support & Contact

CoinhakoSphere is a personal crypto investment journal built for iPhone. Your transactions, watchlist, alerts, and portfolio data live entirely on your device — there is no account, no login, and no remote server holding your information.

If you have a question, found a bug, or want to share feedback, we would love to hear from you. The fastest way to reach us is by email.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CoinhakoSphere connect to any exchange or wallet?

No. CoinhakoSphere is a journaling tool only. It does not connect to exchanges, never asks for wallet credentials, and cannot place trades or move funds.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you record is stored locally on your iPhone. If you enable iCloud Backup on the device, an encrypted copy may be included in your personal iCloud backup — but the app itself never transmits your ledger to a server we operate.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Open Settings inside the app to export your full ledger (transactions, holdings, alerts) as a CSV file at any time.

Does the app send notifications?

No push notifications are sent by CoinhakoSphere. The price alerts you create are evaluated on-device only and never leave your iPhone.

Does CoinhakoSphere provide financial advice?

No. CoinhakoSphere is a journaling and analytics tool. Nothing in the app constitutes investment, tax, or legal advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone to remove all locally stored data. There is no remote account to close because no account was ever created.
